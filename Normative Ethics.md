
* Branch of philosophy concerned with defining what is morally right and wrong, criteria for ethical conduct
* Prescriptive - provide framework for how ppl should act, not just describe how they do act
* Consequentialism (outcomes of an action), deontology (duties and rules) and virtue ethics (character traits)
* Consequentialism - judges morality based on outcomes and consequences, eg. Utilitarianism -> action is right if it  produces greatest amount of good for greatest number of people
* Deontology - morality of action based on whether it adheres to set of rules/duties regardless of consequences
* Virtue ethics - focused on character of the moral agent rather than specific actions or rules - what would a virtuous person do? seeks to cultivate good character traits like honesty, courage and compassion
